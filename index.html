Privacy Policy

Chrome Extension: eBay Actual Sold Price

What it does

This Chrome extension adds a right-click menu option on eBay listing links to open eBay's Terapeak Product Research page with the listing title pre-filled.

Data the extension accesses

Website content (limited): When you right-click an eBay listing link, the extension reads the visible text of that link (typically the listing title) to create the Terapeak search query.

The extension does not read page content unless you trigger it via the context menu.

The extension does not collect browsing history, keystrokes, or analytics data, and it does not access page content beyond what is needed to read the clicked link text.

License activation data

  • License key: You may enter a license key to activate the extension.
  • Device/instance identifier: The extension generates a random identifier on your device to represent that installation for licensing purposes.

How data is stored

License information is stored locally in your browser's extension storage (Chrome storage).

We do not store your license key or device identifier in any website account database unless explicitly stated elsewhere in this policy.

How data is shared

License activation/deactivation requests are sent to Gumroad to manage licensing. Gumroad may receive your license key and device/instance identifier in order to process activation status.

We do not sell or share extension data for advertising, analytics, or unrelated purposes.

Retention

License information remains stored locally until you deactivate the license or remove the extension.

Your choices

You can deactivate the license from the extension's license screen, or uninstall the extension to remove locally stored data.
